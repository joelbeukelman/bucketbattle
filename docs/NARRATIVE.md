# Narrative & World Premise

_Source: user, 2026-10-29. Expand as story decisions are made._

## Premise
A near-future world where **AI has commodified art and craft**. Music is
everywhere, but almost all of it is AI-generated: background music on screens,
in stores, in earbuds. The player is a kid who discovers they can **make and
perform music themselves**, live and by hand. Every battle is a human
playing against the city, other kids, and eventually the machine-made sound.

## Structure
- **Cities are fictional but rooted in real places.** The first city is based
  on NYC; later cities follow the character's background: Tokyo, Dubai,
  London, Bangkok, and more. See ADR-0003.
- **Each level brings a new drum and a new setting.** Levels change the
  instrument (bucket → snare → kit → …) and the place you play it (corner, court,
  subway, rooftop…). Opponents get harder in rhythm complexity *and* in the
  instrument they use.

## Level 1: the bucket corner
- The player leaves **home** and walks toward school.
- On a street corner along the way, a **street performer on buckets** is playing.
  This is the first encounter: it teaches call-and-response and is where
  the player discovers their own talent.
- The middle school is the next landmark. The six school drummers
  (`docs/characters/ROSTER.md`) are the next set of opponents.

## Themes the visuals should carry (see W9)
- **Handmade vs. machine-made.** Human music and its spaces feel warm,
  imperfect, saturated and hand-painted. AI-made content feels cool, uniform,
  glossy and everywhere.
- **Music makes the city come alive.** When the player performs, the
  surroundings respond: lights, passers-by, color.
